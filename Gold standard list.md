
# Sources Used to Construct the Gold-Standard Dataset
Here are listed the academic, conference, technical and preprint sources used to construct the document level gold-standard dataset. These sources were selected because they contain statements, recommendations, policy relevant claims and analytical discussion about EU documents and greener electronics, including circular product design, repairability, waste and end-of-life governance, recycled materials, chemicals and substances of concern, digital product passports, ecodesign, semiconductor sustainability, and broader sustainability regulation in electronics.

The role of these sources was to provide realistic origins for recommendation like queries and statements that could later be mapped to EU legal and policy instruments at a document level. The final gold-standard dataset contains 151 annotated statement-document pairs, corresponding to 130 evaluation queries, drawn from these 20 sources.
